        Two rules that override everything

        Watch the leg, not the back. Leg pain retreating up toward your spine is the response you want, even if your back aches a bit more. Leg pain travelling further down means stop that exercise.

        A nerve is not a muscle. A broad pull in a muscle is a stretch. A thin electric line, burning or pins and needles is the nerve — back off, do not push through, and use the sliders instead.

        Week one: find the direction your back prefers
        Equipment, and what each thing is for
        Why no CrossFit or barbell work in these six months

        Not because you are fragile. With a disc component and five years detrained, CrossFit is the worst-matched format available to you: it combines spinal loading, speed and deliberate fatigue, and form breaks down under fatigue — which is exactly when a disc gets hurt. Your dumbbells and bench cover everything needed for six months of real strength progress, in patterns that keep the spine neutral. Revisit barbell work after week 26, from a much better base, and only if the leg has been quiet for weeks.

        What this programme is built on

        Built for one person with a specific picture: a nine-month sciatic distribution with both a disc and a piriformis component, five years detrained. It is general flexibility, mobility and strength work designed to be safe alongside proper care — not a substitute for a physiotherapist who can watch you move.
